What is a League?

Leagues are opt-in events where players play several matches over a few weeks, earning points and climbing the standings to earn virtual prizes.

Each win in a league earns you 3 points and each loss 1 point, so playing all of your games is always worth it. Your standing is your total points compared with the other participants. Ties are broken by strength of schedule, shown as "Opponent Win %": players who faced strong opponents rank above players with the same points who faced weak ones.

Players may use different decks in different matches, or edit the same deck between matches. When you open or join a table with a deck, that deck is fixed for that game; changes you make afterwards apply to your next game.

Constructed Leagues are events where players use decks they have built from all the cards the league's format allows. These games are like casual matches, but tracked for points.

Sealed Leagues instead hand out virtual sealed product (starter decks and booster packs, as the game was sold), which players open to get the cards they build their decks from. Only the cards from that product can be used in the league. If you join a Sealed League late, don't worry: you get the product from every earlier serie at once.

Solo Draft Leagues let you draft a card pool pack by pack, on your own and at your own pace, and then build a deck from what you drafted.

Each league is made up of one or more Series (usually a week each). Each serie is played in an announced format, and a serie can bring a new format or new sealed product. During a serie you can play a set number of league games, and at most half of them with the same side (Dark or Light), so bring a deck for each side. You cannot play the same opponent twice with the same sides in one serie. The details of each league are on the Events tab under that league's description.

How do I join?

Open the Events tab and look under Current Leagues. Open the league you are interested in and press its Join league button. Some leagues cost a little currency to enter; the price is shown next to the button and is taken from your account when you join.

Once you are in, press Play in the Game Hall and choose Open League Table, or join a waiting league table another participant has opened: a league table shows the league's name in its table info. Refresh your browser if a league you have just joined is not offered yet.

How do I make a Sealed deck?

When you join a Sealed League you are given its product straight away (and more at the start of each later serie). Open the Deck Builder and change the collection list (showing All cards by default) to the name of the league: your packs are shown there, and clicking a pack opens it.

Build your deck as usual from the opened cards, save it, and open a league table from the Game Hall. The league's own cards are kept apart from My Cards; they are for that league only and are not part of your collection once the league is over.

What is the currency for?

The currency shown on My Account is what you get for selling cards you don't need to the Merchant (the button at the top of the Game Hall), where it also buys cards and packs. Some leagues and tournaments ask a small entry fee, which keeps people from entering with several accounts at once; a queue you leave before it starts gives the fee back.

What are the Prizes?

Prizes are virtual cards and packs added to My Cards, which you can use in any deck afterwards (except in the league they were won in: a Sealed League's deck may only use the league's own product).

  1. After your 1st, 3rd, 5th, ... win in a serie, you win one random foil card:
    Win in the seriePrize
    1st, 3rda random foil common (or fixed card)
    5th, 7tha random foil uncommon (or preview card)
    9th, 11th, ...a random foil rare (or premium card)
  2. When the league ends, every player who played at least two league games is given booster packs of their choice, by final standing:
    PositionPrizes
    1st5 Reflections boosters and 5 other boosters
    2nd3 Reflections boosters and 5 other boosters
    3rd1 Reflections booster and 5 other boosters
    4th - 5th5 boosters
    6th - 10th3 boosters
    11th and below1 booster
    "Reflections" means a booster of your choice from Reflections I, II or III; "other" means a booster of your choice from any other set. You pick the sets in the Deck Builder, in My Cards, when you open the packs.

Cheating

Cheating will not be tolerated - period. We are all here to have a good time, and spoiling it for the sake of a few league points is not worth anyone's while.

Cheating includes, but is not limited to:

  1. Entering a league with more than one account.
  2. Entering a Sealed League with several accounts to re-roll your packs until you get something good.
  3. Knowingly exploiting a system or a card bug.
  4. Game fixing: deciding the outcome of a league game in any other way than playing it out.

Players found cheating (or believed to be, at the administrators' discretion) will be removed from the league, and their accounts may be suspended or deleted. This is a friendly environment where everyone wants to have some fun, so don't spoil it for others.

F.A.Q.

  1. Can I trade cards in a Sealed League?
    No. The point of a Sealed League is to give everyone a limited, semi-random card pool to build from, so that deck-building and play skill decide the standings.
  2. When do I get new packs in a Sealed League?
    At the start of each serie, automatically. Open them in the Deck Builder under the league's collection.
  3. I can't open a league table for my side.
    At most half of a serie's games can be played with the same side. Once you have used up your games as Dark (or Light), play the rest with the other side.
  4. Where are my standings?
    On the Events tab, under the league: the standings for the whole league and for each serie, and every match played.
