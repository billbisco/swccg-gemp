League schedules i

A schedule creates leagues on its own: the same settings, an ordered rotation of formats, and a cadence. Each league is created a few days before it starts so players can sign up.
Recurring leagues are not available on this server yet; they come with the league schedules work (slice 5 of the hall port). Until then every league is created by hand with the form below.

The day the first serie opens for play (server time). Players can sign up as soon as the league exists, and the league stays listed for two days after its last serie ends.

Players cannot join by themselves; add them with "Add players to league" at the bottom of this page. The registration info below tells them how to get in.

Collection i
"All cards" lets players build from every card in the game; "My cards" restricts them to the cards they own.
Deck lock-in i
With locking, the first deck a player uses for each side is the one they must keep for the whole league; admins can read the locked decks from the league's page.
Format i
A sealed league has one serie per pack of its product: the days and the match limit apply to each. Players open their packs when they join. A draft league has one serie; players draft their pool from the hall once it has started. The days and the match limit apply to the serie. Each serie runs for the given number of days, one after the other, in its own format; only the first "max matches" ranked games count per player in each.
