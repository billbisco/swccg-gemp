How do I play?

Gemp-Swccg plays the Star Wars Customizable Card Game in your browser: no cards to buy, no table to clear, no clean-up afterwards.

  • New to the game? The SWCCG Wiki explains the rules, the cards and the formats.
  • Build a deck with the Deck Builder, or pick one from the deck library when you open a table: it holds starter and sample decks you can play straight away.
  • Back in the Game Hall, press Play to open a table against another player or against a bot, to join a league table, or to host a tournament. Tables other players have opened are listed under Waiting Tables; press Join table on one.
  • The Manual beside this page walks through the hall, the deck builder and the game window. Format Rules lists what each format allows, and What Are Leagues? explains how league play is scored.

Found a problem? Use Report a Bug at the top of the Game Hall.
